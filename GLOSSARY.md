# Nusa

A notch at the top of a Wayland screen that shows music, alerts and quick tools.

## Language

**Closed notch**:
The small pill shown while the pointer is away: song title, lyric line, alerts, download bar.
_Avoid_: compact view, collapsed notch, peek

**Open notch**:
The large panel shown while the pointer hovers the notch, showing one page at a time.
_Avoid_: expanded notch, popup

**Page**:
One screen of the open notch (home, media, mixer, downloads), switched with the side arrows.
_Avoid_: tab, view, panel

**Lyric line**:
The single current line of synced lyrics shown under the song title in the closed notch.
_Avoid_: subtitle, caption

**Download bar**:
The thin progress bar in the closed notch that tracks a running download.
_Avoid_: progress bar (ambiguous with the song's seek bar)
